## Product Overview
**One-liner:** zema studios does the colour grading of series, films, documentaries, music videos and brand work, from a calibrated room in Lisbon.
**What it does:** Colour grading (finishing the colour of the image) for productions, with the director and the DOP in the room or remotely.
**Product category:** Post-production service: colour grading studio.
**Product type:** Service, per project.
**Business model:** Quote per project (format, duration, camera, delivery date). `[NEED: typical pricing model, day rate or per project]`

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Production companies, platforms and broadcasters, advertising agencies and brands.
**Decision-makers:** Directors of photography, directors, producers and post-production supervisors.
**Primary use case:** Grading a series, a film or a campaign and delivering it to a platform's specs.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get the image the director and DOP intended, consistent across episodes.
- Deliver to platform specs (SDR Rec.709, HDR, DCP) without surprises.
- Grade with the DOP present, or remotely when they can't be.

## Customer Language
**Words to use:** cor, color grading, colorista, grade, sala calibrada, sessão, entrega, diretor de fotografia, realizador, série, temporada.
**Words to avoid:** soluções, experiência única, paixão, elevar, transformar, de excelência, de ponta, "a sua visão", any superlative without proof.

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|-----------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Factual (ofício) | Says what the studio does and how: the room, the process, the deliveries, computed numbers | Boasting, client or brand name-dropping | "Fazemos a cor de séries, filmes e publicidade." |
| Plain | Short declarative Portuguese (pt-PT) | Corporate or lyrical | "A sala é em Lisboa, calibrada." |
| Quiet | The frames carry the page | Taglines, eyebrows | (no slogan) |

**Never use:** client, platform or brand names in marketing copy (Prime Video, RTP, G-SHOCK…): the owner rejected it on 2026-11-22; they stay only in each project's own credits. "zema studios" capitalised (always lowercase); individual names of the team (the studio photo, two colorists seen from behind, is approved); English copy; invented stats, awards or testimonials; text over the graded frames (except the mosaic name chip on hover).
